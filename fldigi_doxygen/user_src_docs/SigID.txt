\page sigid_page Signal Identification (SigID)

\tableofcontents

SigID names the mode of signals in the receive passband, for every mode
fldigi can receive, without an RsID or TxID. It is off by default,
because it uses some CPU: on a recent Mac, about 1.5% of one core for
one signal and about 5% with six signals in the passband.
Turn it on with the SigID button beside RxID, or with
"Identify the signals in the passband" on Configure > IDs/SigID.
The settings below are on that same tab.
<br>

Marks in the receive pane, labels on the waterfall, marks for look-alike
modes, and tuning the rig on a click all start out on. Each has its own
setting, described below.
<br>

\section sigid_marks Marks in the receive pane

Each named signal gets a clickable mark in the receive pane, like an
RsID mark:
<br>

<tt>\<\<2026-10-24T14:02:06Z SigID: THOR22 at 1252 Hz (74%)\>\></tt>
<br>

The time is UTC. The number in parentheses is SigID's confidence.
Clicking the mark switches to that mode and moves to the signal.
With "Clicking a mark tunes the rig so the signal sits at" on, and a
rig fldigi can tune, the dial moves so the signal sits at the audio
frequency beside that control (1500 Hz by default). With that control
off, or with no rig fldigi can tune, only the audio carrier moves.
<br>

"Mark each signal in the receive pane" turns the marks off.
A signal you are already receiving, in that mode and at that frequency,
is not marked and gets no waterfall tab.
<br>

\section sigid_waterfall Labels on the waterfall

A short tab with the mode name is drawn above each named signal. The
tab follows the signal. Clicking a tab does the same as clicking its
mark. "Show labels on the waterfall" turns the tabs off.
<br>

\section sigid_lookalikes Look-alike modes

Some modes cannot be told apart from the spectrum alone. These include
THOR and DominoEX at the same speed, Olivia and Contestia, BPSK and
PSK-R, PSK63 and PSK63F, and others. SigID decodes a few seconds of the
signal to decide. Until then the mark shows both names, as in
<tt>THOR22 or DOMX22</tt>, and the waterfall tab shows
<tt>THOR22/DOMX22</tt>. A click selects the first of the two names.
"Mark look-alike modes before they are told apart" turns those early
marks and tabs off. The signal is then shown only after one mode has
been chosen. A new mark is written when the pair becomes a single mode.
While both names are still shown, a signal you are already receiving
in either of those modes is left unmarked.
<br>

Some groups stay "A or B". SigID has no check yet for MT63 short and
long interleave, MFSK64 and MFSK64L, MFSK128 and MFSK128L, 8PSK with
and without FEC, the multi-carrier PSK modes with and without FEC, and
NAVTEX and SITOR-B. Contestia and Olivia at the slowest speeds, and
THOR Micro and DominoEX Micro, stay a pair as well. With the early
marks turned off, a pair that is never split is not shown.
<br>

\section sigid_settings Other settings

"Lowest confidence to mark (%)" is how far SigID's best match must lead
the next unrelated mode before a signal is marked or labeled. The
percentage in the mark is that figure. The control starts at 10.
<br>

"Mark the same signal again after (seconds)" is the wait before the
same signal, still with the same name, is marked again. The control
starts at 300 seconds.
<br>

\section sigid_limits Limits

SigID works best within a normal 3 kHz passband. It needs a few seconds
of signal, and slow modes need longer. Accuracy drops below about
3 dB SNR. Two signals very close together can be taken as one.
The squelch does not affect it: SigID listens before the squelch.
<br>
